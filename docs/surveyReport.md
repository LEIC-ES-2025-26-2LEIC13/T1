# L.EICo Validation Survey Report

This document presents a detailed analysis of responses collected through a survey designed to validate the relevance and define requirements for a new project management application for L.EIC students. In total, **8 students** participated in the survey, providing useful insights.

---

## Summary

The survey results reveal **consensus** across the L.EIC student community regarding project management challenges:

- **100%** experienced internal disorganization and lack of task clarity
- **100%** encountered internal communication failures  
- **100%** faced technical difficulties during projects

---

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Project Progress Difficulties

**Question:** Have you and your team ever felt stuck in a L.EIC project and weren't quite sure what to do?

**Responses:** 
- "Sometimes" (6)
- "Yes, in almost every project" (2)

**Finding:** All respondents have experienced some difficulties, revealing a significant and persistent problem when planning tasks.


---

### 2. Disorganization and Task Assignment

**Question:** Have you ever found yourself unsure who was doing what or what still needed to be done?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (8/8)

**Finding:** Complete consensus. 
Overall, project organization, work division and task tracking are problematic.

---

### 3. Ineffective Internal Communication

**Question:** Do you think this was due to ineffective internal communication methods?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (8/8)

**Finding:** Absolute consensus across all respondents. Current communication methods fail to keep teams synchronized and organized.

---

### 4. Technical Difficulties with Specific Tasks

**Question:** Have you ever faced a situation where you didn't know how to execute a particular task in a L.EIC project?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (8/8)

**Finding:** All students have encountered technical roadblocks when executing tasks, indicating a need for guided support, although completely normal when facing unseen technical challenges.

---

### 5. Desire for Support

**Question:** Did you feel you needed help from someone who had already completed the project?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (6)
- "No" (2)

**Finding:** 75% of students value empirical knowledge from experienced peers to overcome technical barriers.

Although AI tools and also class materials are useful, guidance from students that already completed the project is relevant and highly desired.

---

### 6. Adoption of Existing Tools

**Question:** Have you used tools like GitHub Projects, Trello, Notion, or similar?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (5)
- "No" (3)

**Finding:** Students have used some of the tools since they are essential for project management, but adoption is not universal.

---

## What Students Appreciated About Existing Tools

**Finding:** More on the coding side, students appreciated Github Project for easier board management and direct integration with code repositories, while Trello and Notion were referenced for their user-friendly interfaces and flexibility in organizing tasks and information.

---

## Frustrations with Current Tools

**Finding:** Most students expressed frustration regarding the lack of features on free versions of tools, essentially limiting their ability to fulfill simple project management needs.

Key features are often locked behind paywalls.

---

## Platform Usage

**Question:** Where do you use these tools?

**Primary Findings:**
- Desktop remains the dominant platform (5 respondents)
- Mobile serves as a secondary platform (2 respondents)

Mobile project management is viewed as a "nice-to-have" feature rather than a primary requirement.

---

## Mobile Application Interest

**Question:** Would you like a way to organize your L.EIC projects collaboratively on your mobile device?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (2)
- "Maybe" (1)
- No response (5)

**Finding:** Moderate interest in mobile functionality, though not critical for the majority of users.

---

## Sources of Help During Projects

**Question:** When you encountered difficulties during the project, how did you get help?

**Primary Sources:**
- AI tools (7 students)
- Peer assistance from other groups (4 students)
- Professor consultation (4 students)
- Web search (3 students)
- Learning from older students (2 students)

**Finding:** Students rely heavily on AI tools and peer collaboration for problem-solving.

---

## Preference for Veteran Guidance

**Question:** Would you prefer to have received help from students who had already completed the project?

**Responses:**
- "Yes" (5)
- "No" (1)
- No response (2)

**Finding:** 83% of respondents prefer guidance from students with previous course experience.

---

## Perceived App Effectiveness

**Question:** Do you think this app would help your team manage the project more effectively?

**Rating Scale (1-10):**
- 10 (4 responses)
- 8 (1 response)
- 7 (2 responses)
- 5 (1 response)

**Average Rating:** 8.4/10

**Finding:** Strong approval for the proposed solution across respondents.

---

## Value of Pre-defined Templates

**Question:** If the app included templates for L.EIC projects with pre-defined tasks, would you find that useful?

**Rating Scale (1-10):**
- 10 (5 responses)
- 9 (1 response)
- 8 (1 response)
- 6 (1 response)

**Average Rating:** 8.9/10

**Finding:** Pre-defined templates are considered a highly valuable feature, particularly for reducing setup time and providing project structure.

---

## Benefits of Template Automation

**Identified Advantages:**
- Reduces initial setup friction
- Accelerates project start
- Provides immediate task clarity
- Facilitates task division among team members
- Prevents confusion from multiple concurrent projects

**Potential Concerns:**
- Over-specification could reduce flexibility
- Students may not understand what aspects to customize
- Risk of template rigidity limiting project uniqueness or future modifications from year to year

---

## Value of Tips in Templates

**Question:** If templates included tips from students who completed previous iterations, would you find this useful?

**Rating Scale (1-10):**
- 10 (5 responses)
- 9 (1 response)
- 4 (1 response)
- 3 (1 response)

**Average Rating:** 7.6/10

**Finding:** Tips are valued, though effectiveness depends heavily on presentation and relevance of the content shared.

---

## Benefits and Risks of Embedded Guidance

**Perceived Benefits:**
- Guidance on avoiding common mistakes
- Practical task execution approaches
- Clear task expectations

**Identified Risks:**
- Tips may become obsolete if project themes change annually
- Risk of contextual irrelevance or confusion
- Over-reliance on past solutions

---

## Desired Additional Features

**Question:** What features would you find valuable in a project management application?

**Identified Feature Requests:**

**Communication Infrastructure**
- Direct messaging between team members
- Automated progress notifications
- Integrated forum or Q&A system
- Mechanisms for integrating solo students into groups

**Project Tracking**
- Real-time task board with progress visualization

**Template Flexibility**
- Toggle Tips on or off (opt-in)
- Access to templates from multiple groups (combining best practices and good approaches)
- Customizable template options

---

## Likelihood of Immediate Adoption

**Question:** If this app was available tomorrow, would you use it for your current L.EIC projects?

**Rating Scale (1-10):**
- 10 (4 responses)
- 9 (1 response)
- 8 (1 response)
- 7 (1 response)
- 2 (1 response)

**Average Rating:** 8.0/10

**Finding:** High likelihood of adoption and team-level conversion for current projects.

---

## Willingness of Veterans to Contribute

**Question:** As a high-achieving student (18+), would you contribute tips to templates for students taking the course later?

**Rating Scale (1-10):**
- 10 (2 responses)
- 8 (1 response)
- 5 (2 responses)
- 4 (1 response)
- 0 (2 responses)

**Average Rating:** 5.3/10

**Finding:** Highly polarized responses indicate that organic community contribution may be insufficient. Incentive mechanisms (gamification, rewards, recognition) are recommended to encourage veteran participation.

---

## Conclusions

The survey data demonstrates:

1. **Universal problems** in current project management approaches among L.EIC students
2. **Strong demand** for a specialized tool addressing collaboration, task clarity, and communication
3. **High receptiveness** to pre-defined templates and structured guidance
4. **Critical need** for incentive systems to sustain knowledge contribution from experienced students
5. **Preference** for peer-led guidance over generic resources (reducing time spent consuming less relevant information not tailored to the specific course context)

These findings strongly support proceeding with application development, with particular emphasis on template quality, tips contribution mechanisms, and collaborative features.

Solo students integration could also be a key feature to consider, although a little bit outside of the original scope of the project, it could be a great way to increase the app's value and adoption.

Forum or Q&A features could also be considered, although they may require more resources to implement and moderate effectively.
